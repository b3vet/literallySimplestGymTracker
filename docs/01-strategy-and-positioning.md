# Strategy & Positioning

> The "why" behind every roadmap decision. If a proposed feature contradicts this doc, the feature is wrong, not the doc.

## The committed positioning (one sentence)
**LS Gym Track is the fastest, most trustworthy strength logger on iOS, with effort-aware (RIR) intelligence delivered on the wrist — minimalist by discipline, honest by design, additive by monetization.**

## The strategic thesis
There is exactly one feature intersection no competitor occupies: **fast + minimalist + RIR-native + watch-first.** Every RIR-strong app lacks a credible Apple Watch; every watch-strong app lacks real RIR; and *no one* uses the Digital Crown to log weight/reps/RIR. We already stand on that intersection. The job is to (a) make the free, fast core undeniable, (b) earn trust no incumbent can buy back, and (c) ship the paid RIR-auto-progression layer on the wrist **before Alpha Progression ships a watch.**

## The watch reframe (the most important mental model)
The Apple Watch is the **#5-of-5** reason people choose a tracker (behind logging speed, free-tier quality, progress visualization, program library). Nobody downloads a gym app *because* it has a watch app. So:

- ❌ Do **not** make "Apple Watch support" the hero of the App Store listing.
- ✅ The crown is the **delivery vehicle** for the two things that *do* pull: **logging speed** (#1 driver) and **RIR on the wrist** (the unoccupied intersection).
- The watch is a **retention + word-of-mouth** lever (wearable integration ≈ +35% 6-month adherence), not an acquisition lever.

## The trust pillar (the through-line)
Three independent evidence lines — honest pricing, free data portability, no retroactive paywalls — all converge on **trust** as our single highest-leverage asset. We have one advantage no incumbent can recover: **no betrayal record.** MyFitnessPal, StrongLifts, GymStreak, and Juggernaut all spent theirs.

**Testable trust claims for the App Store / marketing (not the empty word "minimalist"):**
- "Log a set in one crown-twist — the fastest logging anywhere."
- "RIR on your wrist."
- "Your data exports any time."
- "Your phone is always the source of truth — the watch never overwrites it."
- "One-time purchase, honored forever. No feature ever removed."

## Monetization model (locked direction)
The serious-lifter segment's Reddit-revealed preference, best → worst: **generous free + paid extras (Hevy)** > **lifetime one-time (Strong)** > **annual at ~$10-20** > **monthly ("exploitative for a tracker")**.

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Free tier | **Unlimited core logging + data export, forever** | The acquisition gate Hevy reset to zero; gating it is predicted death |
| Paid shape | **$29.99 lifetime + ~$19.99/year annual. NO monthly at launch.** | Unknown indies must buy trust; monthly is disqualifying for a tracker |
| What's paid | **Additive only:** RIR auto-progression, advanced analytics/trends, HRV readiness | The basic/smart seam — never gate core logging |
| Lifetime rule | **Honored forever; only unlocks additive features; never gates core** | Avoids the StrongLifts "bait and switch" trap; minimal betrayal surface |
| Price stance | Deliberately aggressive (below Alpha/Boostcamp $59.99/yr) | Win the trust-wary indie filter; accept lower ARPU for trust + conversion |

**Honest caveat (do not paper over):** lifetime SKUs under-monetize loyal users and ~40% of lifetime-deal apps fail in 3 years (RevenueCat). The discipline that makes it safe: lifetime unlocks *additive* features only, so even if economics later force a subscription tier, core logging stays free and the betrayal surface is near zero.

## Acquisition reality (the honest risk)
We have **no social viral loop** and **no Android**, so installs come from **generic-term ASO** ("workout tracker", "gym log") + **word-of-mouth-of-craft** — the slowest, most expensive channel, the one Hevy explicitly avoided by building a feed. The addressable paying niche (iOS, serious, RIR-aware, watch-owning, willing-to-pay) is **real but unsized**. Implication: convert a small, high-intent audience extremely well, and treat ASO + a genuinely shareable product as first-class, not afterthoughts.

## The competitive clock ⏰
**Alpha Progression** is the only rival positioned to copy the wedge: it has the best RIR auto-progression, the category's highest satisfaction (4.9★), and a watch app on its roadmap — but its changelog (through v6.1) shows **nothing shipped.** Estimated lead: **~12 months at ~60% confidence; an Alpha watch inside 6 months would collapse the watch-layer advantage.** Monitoring Alpha's watch changelog is the single highest-priority competitive signal. Phase 2 is a race against it.

## Risks, named honestly
1. **Brutal acquisition funnel** — no viral loop, no Android, niche unsized.
2. **The moat is rented** — RIR is free in Boostcamp, core to Alpha; one Alpha watch release from parity.
3. **Strong's stall is the mirror** — the best-loved minimalist logger still got passed by Hevy's velocity. Minimalism must be *actively maintained* with one evolving edge.
4. **The minimalism trap** — the auto-progression/HRV layer could become the bloat we critique. It must stay a single invited nudge, never a coaching surface.
5. **Execution reliability** — the whole trust pillar evaporates if we ship our own data-loss bug. The append-only, phone-as-source-of-truth sync is a strategic asset, not a detail.

## The one bet, restated
Be the fast, honest logger that puts loved-and-evidence-backed **RIR auto-progression on the wrist**, before the one rival who could do the same ships its watch — **but ship humble:** nail trust, speed, and the plate calculator first; give away more than feels comfortable; treat the watch and RIR as the *second* marketing claim, not the first.
