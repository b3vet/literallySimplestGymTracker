## 1. What's New in This Version — `en-US`

**1,340 / 4,000 characters.** Copy everything between the rules.

---
A REDESIGNED POST-WORKOUT SUMMARY
  The screen you see after racking the last bar finally tells you how it went.
  Session volume leads, big, with how it compares to the last time you trained
  this day. New personal records get real space instead of a tiny badge. One bar
  shows how many lifts went up, matched, or slipped. Every lift is a clean row
  you can tap open for the full set-by-set detail.

UPDATE YOUR PROGRAM IN ONE TAP
  Beat your targets? The summary now gathers every lift worth bumping into a
  single card, so you can raise them all at once or one at a time. And the fix
  you asked for: once you apply one, it stays applied. No more buttons resetting
  themselves while you scroll.

DELETE A WORKOUT
  Logged a session by mistake, or finished one you would rather forget? Swipe it
  away in History and confirm. Your records, stats, and vs-last-time comparisons
  all recalculate immediately.

PLATE CALCULATOR REMOVED
  The per-side plate breakdown under the weight picker is gone, along with the
  bar weight and plate inventory settings. It had no way of knowing which of
  your lifts actually use a barbell, so it kept showing loading that did not
  apply to the movement in front of you. A number you cannot trust sitting next
  to your working weight is worse than no number at all.

Thanks for lifting with me :)
---

**On the removal wording.** It is named plainly, the reason is given in the
lifter's terms, and it does not promise a return — Berke's position was "it is
not a path that I will want to follow". Saying nothing would be worse: users who
relied on it will notice, and an unexplained disappearance reads as a bug. The
paragraph is last so the release leads with what was gained.

Format follows 1.3.0's own What's New: all-caps section headers, two-space
indented body, warm sign-off.

## 5. App Review notes

Paste into **App Review Information → Notes**:

---
No account or login is required. The app is entirely offline — all data is stored locally on device in SQLite. There is no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDK, so there is nothing for the reviewer to sign in to.

To exercise the app: open it, complete the short onboarding (pick a unit and accent, then choose a starter program template), tap START WORKOUT, pick a day, and log a set. Finishing the workout shows the redesigned post-workout summary, which is the main change in this version. History supports swipe-to-delete on a session, with a confirmation dialog.

Apple Watch: the watchOS companion is optional and is not required to review the iPhone app. It uses HealthKit to run a workout session (heart rate and active energy) so that the watch can record a real workout and close activity rings. HealthKit data is read and written only during an active workout and never leaves the device. The usage strings are NSHealthShareUsageDescription and NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription in the watch app's Info.plist.

The app also uses a Live Activity / Dynamic Island widget to show the current exercise and rest countdown during a workout. It can be turned off in Settings.

Changes in 1.4.1: the post-workout summary was redesigned, deleting a workout from History was added, and the per-side plate calculator was removed along with its bar weight and plate inventory settings.
---

Contact info: use whatever is already on file. The support URL on the listing is
`https://b3vet.github.io/lsworkouttracker-site/`.
