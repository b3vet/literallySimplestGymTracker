## 1. What's New in This Version — `en-US`

About 1,230 / 4,000 characters. Copy everything between the rules.

---
COPY A PROGRAM OR A DAY
  Want to try a heavier block without losing the one that works? Open a
  program or a day and tap the copy button. You get an exact, independent copy
  (every day, lift, target and drop set) and changes to it never touch the
  original. Your records and stats come along, because a lift is the same lift
  in every program.

EXERCISE VARIANTS
  The smith machine is taken again? Give any lift in your program its
  variants, like a machine or dumbbell version, each with its own target
  weight. Mid-workout, open the exercise menu (top left) and switch in one
  tap. Your remaining sets carry over and the weight is already right.
  Records, stats and targets are tracked per variant, because a machine PR is
  not a barbell PR.

SMARTER SUMMARY
  Swapped a lift mid-workout? The summary now offers to save it as a variant
  of that slot instead of adding it as a new exercise. And the first time you
  ever do a movement it says FIRST TIME instead of awarding you a personal
  record you did not have to beat.

DELETE OLD PROGRAMS SAFELY
  Deleting a program or a day you have trained keeps every workout you logged
  with it. They stay in History with all their sets.

Thanks for lifting with me :)
---

Format follows 1.3.0 / 1.4.1: all-caps section headers, two-space indented
body, warm sign-off. The last section describes a fix: before 1.5.0, deleting a
program with logged workouts failed. It is worded as a guarantee, not as a bug
fix.

## 5. App Review notes

Paste into **App Review Information → Notes**:

---
No account or login is required. The app is entirely offline — all data is stored locally on device in SQLite. There is no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDK, so there is nothing for the reviewer to sign in to.

To exercise the app: open it, complete the short onboarding (pick a unit and accent, then choose a starter program template), tap START WORKOUT, pick a day, and log a set.

New in 1.5.0: in Programs, open a program or a day and tap the copy icon (top right) to duplicate it. To try exercise variants: open a day, tap an exercise, tap VARIANTS, add one (for example "Shoulder Press (Machine)") with a weight. Start that day's workout, tap the tune button at the top left of the workout screen, and choose Switch variant.

Apple Watch: the watchOS companion is optional and is not required to review the iPhone app. It uses HealthKit to run a workout session (heart rate and active energy) so that the watch can record a real workout and close activity rings. HealthKit data is read and written only during an active workout and never leaves the device. The usage strings are NSHealthShareUsageDescription and NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription in the watch app's Info.plist.

The app also uses a Live Activity / Dynamic Island widget to show the current exercise and rest countdown during a workout. It can be turned off in Settings.
